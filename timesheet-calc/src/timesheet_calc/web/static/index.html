      How times are read
      8 or 12
      One or two digits are whole hours: 8:00, 12:00.
      130 or 1500
      Three or four digits are hours and minutes: 1:30, 15:00.
      7.15
      A period works like a colon. What follows is minutes, so 7.5 is 7:05, not 7:30.
      12:10 to 3
      With "Guess AM/PM", an end earlier than the start is read as PM (2:50), or as overnight if that still doesn't fit. With "24-hour only", it is always overnight (14:50).
      6 to 6
      Rejected: it could mean 0, 12, or 24 hours. Enter 6 to 18, or 0 to 24 for a full day.
